FAQ

Questions we get before every project

How long does an installation take?

Most single-room projects are finished in one visit. Larger projects are split into scheduled days, confirmed with you before we start.

Do you work with property managers and rental owners who live abroad?

Yes. We can run the whole process remotely — assessment by video, approval by message, and photos of the finished result when we hand the keys back.

Do the panels work with Alexa or Google Home?

The systems we install are app-controlled and integrate with Alexa and Google Home. We set the integration up and save your scenes before we leave.

Will it damage the wall or ceiling?

We plan mounting and wiring during the assessment so the install is clean and reversible wherever possible, and we tell you upfront if a surface needs patching afterwards.

Which areas do you serve?

The Orlando metro area and surrounding Central Florida communities. Ask about your address — if it is outside our usual radius we will tell you before quoting.

Is there a warranty?

Fixtures carry their manufacturer warranty, and our installation work is covered as stated in your quote. Ask us for the exact terms for your project.
