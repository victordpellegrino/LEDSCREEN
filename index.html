Where we install

One screen or a whole wall of them

The right screen depends on where people sit, how much light hits the wall and what plays on it. That is what the assessment is for.

Homes & media rooms

Living rooms, home theaters and bedrooms: screen sized to the seating distance, wiring inside the wall, no box of cables on the floor.

Lanai, patio & pool

Weather-rated outdoor screens bright enough for daylight under the pool cage, with mounts and enclosures built for Florida sun and rain.

Vacation rentals & game rooms

Game rooms, themed bedrooms and lounges that photograph well for the listing — and survive guests, because the mount is rated for it.

Business & signage

Storefront displays, menu boards, kiosks, sports bars and lobby video walls — including multi-screen layouts driven from one source.

Mounting

The mount matters as much as the screen

Drywall, stucco, concrete block and glass each need a different anchor. Tell us the wall and what the screen has to do, and we bring the right hardware.

Fixed / flush

Screen sits tight to the wall. The cleanest look, best when the seating is straight in front.

Tilt

Angles down from a high position — above a fireplace, a bar or in a garage gym.

Full-motion (articulating)

Pulls out, swivels and tilts. For corners, kitchens, lanais and rooms with more than one viewing spot.

Mobile cart

Rolls from room to room and locks in place. Good for events, training rooms and anything temporary.

Floor stand / pedestal

No holes in the wall. The answer for rentals, glass walls and stone the owner will not let us drill.

Ceiling / suspended

Dropped from the ceiling or rigged over a bar, a counter or an open space with no usable wall.

Recessed

Built into the wall so the face sits flush with the drywall. Planned before the finish work.

Video wall & structure

Multiple panels on a frame or a freestanding structure, aligned flat and driven as one screen.

Indoor vs outdoor

Same resolution outside, different build

Outdoor does not mean a coarse, low-resolution screen. We install fine pitch — P1, P2 and P3 — outdoors too, in IP-rated sealed cabinets. What really changes is brightness and weather protection.

Indoor

Pixel pitchP1 to P3 — fine pitch for close viewing
BrightnessAround 600–1,500 nits
ProtectionStandard cabinet
Typical useMedia rooms, lobbies, bars, signage

Outdoor

Pixel pitchP1, P2 and P3 too, in IP-rated cabinets
Brightness5,000–10,000+ nits for daylight
ProtectionSealed cabinet, IP-rated for sun and rain
Typical useLanai, pool, patio, storefronts, stages

Rule of thumb: multiply the pixel pitch in millimetres by 10 and you get the minimum comfortable viewing distance in feet. A P2.5 screen looks sharp from about 25 ft; a P6 wall wants 60 ft. Pitch follows the viewing distance — not whether the screen is inside or outside.

FAQ

Asked before almost every project

Can I put a regular TV outside on the lanai?

Under a fully covered lanai some people do, but an indoor TV is not sealed against humidity or insects and is rarely bright enough once the sun hits it. Outdoor-rated screens exist for exactly that spot — we will tell you honestly when the cheaper option is fine and when it will fail.

Do I need an LED video wall or just a big screen?

Up to about 110 inches a single screen is usually cheaper and simpler. Beyond that, or when the image must be seen in daylight or in an odd shape, LED panels make more sense — including fine pitch P1 to P3 outdoors, in IP-rated cabinets. The form below tells us which one your space needs.

Do you install a screen I already bought?

Yes. Install-only is one of the options in the form — send the model and we quote the mount, the anchors and the labour.

Can you hide the cables inside the wall?

On drywall, almost always. On concrete block or stucco we run a surface conduit painted to match, or plan a route through an adjacent space. We agree on the approach before we drill anything.

How long does it take?

A single screen on an existing outlet is usually a same-visit job. New wiring, outdoor structures and video walls are scheduled over more than one day, confirmed with you in advance.

Which areas do you cover?

The Orlando metro area and nearby Central Florida — Kissimmee, Davenport, Clermont, Windermere, Winter Garden and around. Send your address and we will confirm before quoting.

What does it cost?

A wall-mounted screen is a few hundred dollars of work; an LED video wall is priced per square foot and rises steeply as the pixel pitch gets finer. That is why the quote comes after the assessment — we would rather give you a real number than a range that means nothing.
